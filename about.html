Who We Are

Building Trusted Partnerships Through Responsible Metal Recycling

Prime Kingdom Exim Pvt Ltd is an international scrap metal recycling, processing, and trading company committed to supplying quality ferrous and non-ferrous scrap materials to industrial customers worldwide. We work closely with trusted suppliers and logistics partners to deliver dependable recycling solutions that support modern manufacturing and responsible resource utilization.

Our operations focus on efficient material sourcing, careful inspection, quality verification, and timely delivery. Every shipment is managed with attention to consistency, transparency, and customer requirements, enabling businesses to procure recyclable metals with confidence and reliability.

At Prime Kingdom Exim Pvt Ltd, we believe long-term business relationships are built through integrity, professional service, and sustainable practices. By combining industry expertise with a customer-first approach, we continue to support manufacturers, foundries, recyclers, and industrial partners across international markets.
