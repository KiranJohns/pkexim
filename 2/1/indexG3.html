Step-by-Step Lifecycle

The Scrap Transformation Journey

Step 01

Industrial Collection

Direct procurement of raw metallic waste and scrap from heavy manufacturing facilities, demolition sites, and industrial complexes using heavy-duty fleet operations.

Step 02

Material Inspection

Rigorous on-site evaluation using spectro-analyzers to verify elemental metallurgy, contamination levels, and structural grade consistency prior to acceptance.

Step 03

Precision Sorting

Advanced mechanical and optical sorting techniques separating ferrous metals from non-ferrous alloys such as aluminium, copper, brass, and stainless steel.

Step 04

Processing & Shearing

Heavy industrial baling, shredding, and hydraulic shearing to reduce high-density metal structures into standardized furnace-ready dimensions.

Step 05

Quality Testing

Final verification ensuring zero radiation risks, precise density thresholds, and conformity with international ISRI (Institute of Scrap Recycling Industries) standards.

Step 06

Industrial Packaging

Bundle strapping, palletizing, and bulk loading organized systematically to maximize payload volume and streamline downstream port discharge.

Step 07

Container Stuffing

Precision weight balancing and certified stuffing into 20ft and 40ft heavy-duty sea containers equipped with real-time tracking sensors.

Step 08

Intermodal Logistics

Seamless overland transit from processing yards to ocean ports utilizing our dedicated logistics fleet and partner rail lines.

Step 09

Global Ocean Export

Customs documentation, ocean freight booking, and final delivery to partner foundries, steel mills, and smelters globally.
